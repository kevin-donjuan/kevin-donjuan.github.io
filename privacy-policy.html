1. Purpose of this app

Invoice App is a private, internal business tool used solely by Briteway Fleet Washing Services, LLC ("Briteway") to access and manage Briteway's own QuickBooks Online company data through Intuit's official API. It is not offered, sold, or distributed to the public or to any third party.

2. Information the app accesses

Through the QuickBooks Online accounting scope, the app accesses only Briteway's own business records, including company information, customer records, products and services, invoices, and file attachments that Briteway uploads to its own invoices. The app does not access consumer data of any other business or individual.

3. How information is used

Information is used exclusively for Briteway's own bookkeeping and invoicing operations: creating invoices, attaching Briteway's invoice documents to them, and sending invoices to Briteway's customers at Briteway's direction.

4. Sharing

Briteway does not sell, rent, or share QuickBooks data with third parties for marketing or any other purpose. Data passes only between Briteway, Intuit (as the QuickBooks platform provider), and the Muse platform operated by Meta, which provides the automation tooling Briteway uses to run this app.

5. Security and storage

Access to QuickBooks is granted through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. OAuth credentials are stored in encrypted secure-vault storage provided by the Muse platform and are never stored in plain text or shared. Connections use encrypted (HTTPS) transport.

6. Retention and revocation

Data is retained only as part of Briteway's own QuickBooks company records, under Briteway's control. Briteway may revoke this app's access at any time from within QuickBooks Online or the Intuit developer portal, which immediately ends the app's access.

7. Changes and contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to kevin@britewayfleetwash.com. Updates to this policy will be posted at this address.
